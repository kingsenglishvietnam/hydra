## KNOWN TRAPS

- A registered provider **locks its own DLL**. Always unregister → build →
  register.
- `-Register` recreates the listener key empty, so credentials must be re-set
  every time.
- `Restart-Service TermService -Force` fails while the Hydra service is running,
  and separately trips over `UmRdpService` being Manual/Stopped. `rdsprov-register.ps1`
  now stops and starts `TermService` on its own.
- `Select-String` on `bcdedit`/`hydractl` output silently misses matches unless
  piped through `Out-String -Width 200` first.
- `cd X; [IO.File]::ReadAllText('rel.txt')` resolves the relative path in the
  OLD directory — PowerShell parses the whole line first. Use absolute paths.
- Patch anchors must tolerate `\r?\n`; `hydrardp.c` and now the provider sources
  have mixed line endings from these scripts.
