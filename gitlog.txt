HANDOFF.md: assembled from every prior session. Recovers material that existed
only in conversation and would have been lost:

- D3D11 shared textures do NOT cross a Terminal Services session boundary
  (OpenSharedResourceByName -> E_INVALIDARG). This is why capture runs inside
  the seat session and frames travel as CPU pixels through the seqlock ring.
  The original IDD topology was abandoned for this reason, not arbitrarily.
- IDDCX_ADAPTER_FLAGS_REMOTE_SESSION_DRIVER exists, but the OS fails init if
  the flag and the RD stack's involvement disagree. The RD stack must
  instantiate the driver -- which is the entire justification for HydraProto.
- The 08-12 trace shows GetHardwareId IS called (count=200), then
  ConnectNotify session=4, then PreDisconnect reason=17. Combined with error
  87 this reads as: stack asked, driver failed to install, connection dropped.
  Earlier notes saying the stack never asks are wrong.
- EnableWddmIdd is termsrv TELLING the provider its mode, not asking. Ruled
  out as the cause of the silence.
- build-driver.ps1 had three doubled-quoting bugs; -Remote had never taken
  effect and both driver variants were byte-identical.
- hydra_ipc.h publishes a 'stalled' field -- PROBLEM 1's first candidate,
  built and then never read during an actual lockup.
- Monitor separation was ~8100px (0,0 and 11340,0); the reset put them at
  3240,0 adjacent. That separation, not ClipCursor, was the cursor-leak fix.
- HydraProto emergency undo: delete the WinStations key, restart TermService.

=== 98ad249 2026-08-11
hydra: fix the /gfx crash, CPU spin and video glitching

Root cause of the crash was update->DesktopResize being NULL. gdi_graphics_pipeline_init sends ResetGraphics on attach, the server answers with a desktop-size notification, and libfreerdp calls update->DesktopResize(gdi->context) having checked only that update itself is non-NULL: call *0x68(%r14) at libfreerdp3+0xDCB4B, one argument, offsetof confirmed 0x068. Without /gfx that path never runs, which is why eleven earlier hypotheses about codecs, surfaces and pointers all missed it. Found with rdp/hydra_veh.c, a first-chance vectored handler that resolves [rsp+00] to module+RVA and names the caller directly. Two further faults fixed on top: the publish throttle now tests the clock at function entry, since the main loop spins at ~4100/s under gfx and 98 percent of calls did their region bookkeeping before being discarded (CPU ~200 percent to normal); and publishing is now driven by the gfx EndFrame callback rather than a blind 16ms timer, which could sample mid-blit and emit part-new part-old frames. That last one was the glitching. Also retires both HYDRA_GFX bisect guards, which were scaffolding for a bug that was somewhere else entirely.
